How it works

One number, from first message to the door.

  1. They message you

    From Google Maps, Instagram or a missed call. The bot reads “2 shawarma and a juice” and matches it to your menu, in English or Arabic.

  2. They check out

    A menu link opens with the cart filled. Add-ons, a map pin for the door, then card, cash or card machine on delivery.

  3. Your kitchen accepts

    Orders land on a live kitchen screen. One tap to accept with a ready time; the customer is told on WhatsApp.

  4. They watch it arrive

    Your rider’s phone shares GPS. The customer sees the bike move and gets “arriving now” when it’s close.
